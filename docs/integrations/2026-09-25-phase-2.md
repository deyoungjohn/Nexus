## P2.4 — Earnings/event source

For the MVP, **issuer investor-relations announcements are the confirmation
source**, entered manually with direct announcement URL, retrieval time,
announced event time and IANA timezone, confirmation state and expiry. Primary
sources checked include [NVIDIA Investor Relations](https://investor.nvidia.com/home/),
[AMD's IR calendar](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/ir-calendar), and
[Apple's earnings page](https://investor.apple.com/earnings/default.aspx).
An empty upcoming calendar means no confirmed event; historical cadence or an
algorithmic estimate cannot fill the gap. Recheck the announcement at planning
and approval; a shifted, withdrawn or ambiguous date suspends the dependent
rule. The labelled demonstration may still use user-supplied records, but must
not present them as issuer-confirmed events.

The Binance stock guide says earnings/news need an additional data source;
its asset status can indicate a current restriction but does not provide a
reliable future event calendar. No automated feed or scraping license has been
selected. Automated date-dependent decisions remain blocked until the P5/P6
event adapter and revision checks are built. See
[ADR 0003](../decisions/0003-p2-integration-gates.md).

## P2 exit assessment

- **Verified for feasibility:** exact BSC settlement and three Ondo candidate
  identities/18-decimal contracts; public API fields and missing timestamp;
  raw versus scaled wallet quantity on an actual NVDAon receipt; quote and
  exact sender/spender/recipient/calldata/minimum/deadline; wallet approval
  and swap previews; one owner-authorized mainnet transaction with successful
  receipt, transfer logs and post-trade balance/allowance reconciliation;
  issuer IR chosen as the manual confirmed-event source.
- **Still required for production:** owner-specific issuer eligibility,
  trustworthy underlying-reference freshness, supported token audit or a
  documented exception policy, per-asset quantity/transfer checks for AMDon
  and AAPLon, automated revalidation, durable reservations, idempotency,
  failure/reorg recovery and calendar revision handling. P5/P7 own these
  gates; the application execution adapter remains disabled.
- **Result:** P2 feasibility is **complete**. The single supervised NVDAon
  trade is evidence for adapter design, not permission to turn on automated
  trading or to allowlist the other candidate instruments.
