# ADR 0000: Decision title

- Status: proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: human and/or agent

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and evidence that make a decision necessary.

## Decision

State the chosen approach precisely.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, risks, compatibility effects, and follow-up work.

## Alternatives considered

List serious alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Verification

State how the decision will be validated or revisited.
